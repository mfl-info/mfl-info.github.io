TOURNAMENT

Match Rules

1. Team & Players

  • Each team consists of a maximum of 7 players.
  • 6 players, including the goalkeeper, are allowed on the field at a time.

2. Goalkeeper Rules

  • The goalkeeper cannot be changed during the match.
  • Goalkeeper changes are allowed only during a penalty shootout (tiebreaker), subject to the Captain's decision.
  • The goalkeeper may throw or kick the ball from within the penalty area.

3. Match Duration

  • Group Stage: 10 + 10 minutes.
  • Semi-Final: 15 + 15 minutes.
  • Final: 25 + 25 minutes.
  • Time Added: 5 minutes of additional time will be added to every match.
  • Half-Time Break: 5 minutes.

4. Substitution

Substitutions can be made only when the ball is out of play and the match is paused.

5. Fouls & Penalties

  • The Referee's decision is final and binding.
  • Any fighting or disorderly conduct may lead to Team Disqualification.

6. Disciplinary Actions

  • If a player receives 2 Yellow Cards, they will be shown a Red Card and must leave the field immediately.
  • A player receiving a Red Card will be suspended for the next match only. However, they will be eligible to play in the Final.

7. Semi-Final Qualification & Tie-Breaker Rules

Qualification: The top 2 teams from each group will directly qualify for the Semi-Finals.

Tie-Breaker Order (If Points Are Equal):

  • Goal Difference (GD): Team with the higher goal difference.
  • Head-to-Head: Result of the match between the tied teams.
  • Fair Play Record: Team with fewer Yellow/Red Cards.
  • Penalty Shootout / Tiebreaker Match: If teams remain tied in all criteria, a penalty shootout or short tiebreaker match will decide the qualifier.

8. Dugout Rules

  • Only players and team officials are allowed in the dugout.
  • No one from the dugout is permitted to enter the field during play.

9. Attendance / Walkover

If a team fails to arrive by the scheduled time, they will be considered defeated. The team present on time will be declared the winner by Walkover.

10. Results & Points

Win = 3 Points  |  Draw = 1 Point  |  Loss = 0 Points.

11. Knockout Tie-Breaker

A penalty shootout (tiebreaker) will be applicable for all knockout stage matches.

12. Referee Objection Rule

If a team has a valid objection regarding a referee, they must submit a written request with appropriate reasons to the Organizing Committee at least 1 hour before the match. The committee will change the referee if the objection is found to be reasonable.

13. Handball Policy

The distinction between an accidental (unintentional) handball and a deliberate handball rests entirely upon the Referee's discretion. The Referee's decision in this regard shall be final and binding.

14. Ball In & Out

During a throw-in, opponents must maintain a distance of at least 2 meters from the thrower.

15. Footwear Code

  • Hard studs or metal cleats are strictly prohibited.
  • Only football socks (anklet/football socks) are allowed.

16. Linesman & Referee Consultation Rule

  • Linesman Assistance: Linesmen (Assistant Referees) assist the Main Referee regarding ball out-of-bounds, fouls, and incidents outside the Referee's direct view.
  • Consultation: If the Main Referee is unsure about a situation or misses an incident, they have the right to consult with the Linesman before making a decision.
  • Final Authority: The Main Referee retains final authority. Their decision after consultation is final and binding on all teams.

17. Goal Celebration & Field Protection Policy

  • Prohibited Actions: Taking off jerseys during goal celebrations or causing damage to field property, such as nets, poles, boundary lines, or turf, is strictly prohibited.
  • Disciplinary Action: Any violation will result in severe disciplinary action. The Referee may issue an immediate Yellow Card or Red Card depending on the severity of the offense.
  • Financial Penalty: If any field equipment or property is damaged, the responsible player or team must pay the full cost of the damages.

18. Team Supplies & Responsibility Policy

  • Self-Arrangement: Each team is strictly responsible for arranging its own drinking water, food, bottles, towels/handkerchiefs, and other personal items during the tournament.
  • Prohibition: Neither the Organizing Committee nor spectators/outsiders will provide or supply personal items or refreshments to any team or dugout.
  • Disciplinary Action: Any dispute, argument, or blame directed at the Committee regarding these supplies will result in immediate and strict disciplinary action against the team or individual involved.

19. Authority Rules

  • The Authority reserves the right to change any rules at any time.
  • The Referee's decision is final in all circumstances.
  • All teams must adhere to the tournament's discipline and regulations.
COMPETITION POINTS

Points & Walkover

Normal Match

Win = 3 points

Draw = 1 point

Loss = 0 points

Walkover

Walkover win = 3 points

For the table, a walkover may be recorded as 3–0 unless the official competition committee publishes a different ruling.

Goals do not give separate points. GF, GA and GD are used for standings and tie-breaks.
