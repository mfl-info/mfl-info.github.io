Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about registration, fees and payment.

Who can play in MFL?

MFL is only for the boys of Mosjidpara, aged 12 to 25. Applicants from outside Mosjidpara, or younger than 12 or older than 25, are not allowed.

When does registration open?

Registration opens on 16 December 2026 at 12:00 AM, Bangladesh time. You can look at the form before then, but you cannot send it.

How much does it cost?

The fee is for a player and for a captain. You pay only after the management team approves your application.

How do I pay?

After approval, open the Status page with your Application ID and phone number. Send the fee by Send Money with bKash or Nagad to the number shown there, then enter the TrxID from your SMS. Only pay to the number shown on the Status page.

What happens after I apply?

You get an Application ID. The management team calls the phone number you gave to check your details. If you are approved, the payment option opens on your Status page. After the committee checks your payment, your registration is confirmed.

I lost my Application ID. What should I do?

Contact the organisers from the Contact page. Please keep a screenshot of your ID when you apply.

Can I get a refund?

The refund policy will be announced by the management committee before registration opens. If you have a question before you pay, contact the organisers first.

How are teams formed?

Teams are formed by lottery (draw). The lottery date will be announced on the Updates page.

Who can see my information?

Only the MFL management committee. Read the privacy note on the Contact page.

Still stuck? Contact the organisers.
