Points Rules

Win = 3 points · Draw = 1 point · Loss = 0 points.

Goals do not give separate points. GF, GA and GD are used for standings tie-breaks.

Walkover win = 3 points and is recorded as 3–0 unless the competition committee publishes another ruling.
